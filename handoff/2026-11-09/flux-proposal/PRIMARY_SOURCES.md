(2005), A&A 444, 505–519, [primary research PDF](https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~shuleli/0706/orlando2005.pdf), DOI [10.1051/0004-6361:20052896](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20052896). No equation is claimed independently read from that paper. The harmonic law is supported by the directly read Orlando primary implementation and is explicitly defined as the proposed new isotropic model.
